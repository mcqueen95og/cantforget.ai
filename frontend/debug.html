Debug Metrics

  • Detection (ms): Time for YuNet face detection per frame.
  • Tracking (ms): Time for centroid tracker update.
  • Recognize (ms): Time for SFace embedding + matching per face.
  • Similarity score: Cosine similarity 0–1. Higher = more confident match.
  • Model version: Embeddings are tagged with model:dim. Incompatible models are never compared.
  • Landmarks: 5 facial landmarks (eyes, nose, mouth corners) used for alignment.

Privacy: No raw embeddings or biometric data are displayed or logged. Only similarity scores and timing metrics are shown.
